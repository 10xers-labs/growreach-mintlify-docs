# Quickstart

Get GrowReach up and running in under 10 minutes.

## 1. Create your account

1. Go to [dashboard.growreach.app/login](https://dashboard.growreach.app/login) and sign up.
2. Choose your plan (or start your 7-day free trial).
3. Verify your email.

## 2. Connect your LinkedIn account

1. From the dashboard, go to **Settings → LinkedIn Accounts**.
2. Click **Connect Account** and authorize LinkedIn.
3. Your account appears in the list once connected.

<Tip>
  Each plan includes a set number of LinkedIn accounts (1 on Starter, 1 on Serious, 1 on Committed). You can add more accounts as an add-on.
</Tip>

## 3. Set your targets

1. Go to **Targets**.
2. **Accounts to monitor** — add the LinkedIn profiles whose posts you want to engage with (creator targeting).
3. **Keywords** — add the topics and keywords relevant to your niche (keyword targeting).
4. Set your **Topic Filters** to skip posts about religion, politics, personal updates, and health updates.

See [Setup Targets](/guides/setup-targets) for the full guide.

## 4. Make your first comment

1. Go to **Posts** — GrowReach shows you the most relevant, high-engagement posts.
2. Review the suggested comment.
3. Either **copy and post it manually**, or switch on **Auto Mode** to post automatically.

## 5. Teach GrowReach your voice (optional but recommended)

Set up your [Commenting Voice](/guides/commenting-voice) so comments sound like you — tone, persona, emoji preference, and custom instructions.
