# Quickstart

Get GrowReach up and running in under 10 minutes.

## 1. Create your account

1. Go to [https://dashboard.growreach.app/start](https://dashboard.growreach.app/start).
2. Fill in your **Email**, **Confirm Email**, **Password**, and **Confirm Password**.
3. Click **Sign Up**.

<Note>
  There is no separate consent checkbox — by signing up you accept GrowReach's [Terms of Use](https://growreach.app/terms-of-use), [EULA](https://growreach.app/eula), and [Privacy Policy](https://growreach.app/privacy-policy).
</Note>

## 2. Find your LinkedIn profile

You'll land on the profile setup step:

1. Type your **full name** or paste your **LinkedIn profile URL** into the search box.
2. Click **Search**.
3. When it resolves, your profile card appears with your name and LinkedIn URL — click **Continue**.

## 3. Set your professional background

The next step asks for your professional information:

- **Your Full Name**
- **Your Job Role Or Professional Title** (select from the list)
- **Domain/Industry You Work In** (select from the list)
- **Business/Product/Company Name**
- **Total Years Of Experience**
- **Seniority Level** (select from the list)
- **Your Domain Expertise/Skills** (comma-separated — e.g. SEO, Digital Marketing, Paid Ads)
- **A Short Summary Of Your Professional Background** — *optional, auto-generated by GrowReach AI from your LinkedIn profile. Wait a few seconds for it to fill, then edit as you like.*

Click **Continue**.

<Tip>
  An accurate persona is the foundation of good AI comments. The [My AI Persona](/commenting/my-ai-persona) page lets you refine this later.
</Tip>

## 4. Choose your commenting mode

Pick how GrowReach handles comments:

- **Manual** — GrowReach emails you posts with a recommended comment; you approve before anything is posted.
- **Auto** — GrowReach generates and posts comments automatically, in your timezone's normal usage hours.

Select your mode and click **Set Commenting Mode**.

## 5. Connect your LinkedIn account

Connect your LinkedIn account with GrowReach using either method:

- **Chrome Extension** — install our browser extension and connect in one click (works with Google Chrome and Brave Browsers, not Firefox yet).
- **LinkedIn Credentials** — sign in to LinkedIn as usual and connect your account.

See [Connected Accounts](/team-management/connected-accounts) for the full walkthrough.

## 6. (Optional) Set your targets

- **Target LinkedIn Users** — add specific accounts to engage with (up to 20). [Learn more](/commenting/target-linkedin-users)
- **Target Topics** — add the keywords and topics you want GrowReach to find posts on (up to 5). [Learn more](/commenting/target-topics)

You can also choose **Do It Later** and set these up from the sidebar anytime.

## 7. You're in the dashboard

Once connected you land on your **Dashboard** — a real-time snapshot of your growth, engagement, and activity across LinkedIn. Take the **Quick Tour** (9 steps for individuals, 7 for managers) to get familiar.
