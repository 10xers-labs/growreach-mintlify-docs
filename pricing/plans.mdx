## Which plan is right for you?

- **Starter ($19)** — entry-level. For freelancers, consultants, coaches, employees building a personal brand, founders, and B2B sales reps getting started with LinkedIn engagement.
- **Serious ($39)** — 3x more target accounts and 3x more comments for a small price increase. The obvious choice for people who are serious about consistent engagement.
- **Committed ($79)** — the full feature set including keyword targeting. For users who need financial ROI from LinkedIn commenting: agencies, SDRs/BDRs, founders, consultants, coaches, and marketers.
