# Boost My Post

Boost Post increases engagement on a **specific LinkedIn post** by automatically adding comments and interactions through GrowReach — in a controlled and safe way.

> **Sidebar label:** Boost My Post • **Route:** `/boost-post`

## How it works

1. Paste the **LinkedIn post URL** you want to boost into the input (*"Paste the post link you want to boost"*).
2. Click **Validate Link** — GrowReach confirms the post is accessible.
3. Select the profiles to engage with your post — **Select All** or pick the ones that fit your professional domain.
4. GrowReach drives comments and interactions on the post to give it visibility.

<Tip>
  Need the post URL? Use the **"How to copy LinkedIn profile URL?"** help link under the input.
</Tip>

## What makes a boost effective

GrowReach recommends **selecting all profiles (or at least within your professional domain)** so your growth feels natural and stays aligned with your niche:

- **Build Authentic Engagement** — when your 1st-degree connections react or comment on your posts, LinkedIn recognizes it as organic and high-quality.
- **Algorithm visibility** — authentic engagement signals LinkedIn to boost your content's reach beyond your immediate network to 2nd-degree connections. A post with 3-5 substantive comments within an hour gets distributed faster.
