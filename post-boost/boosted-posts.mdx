# Boosted Posts

Boosted Posts is where you review **current and past boost activity** for all your boosted posts.

> **Sidebar label:** Boosted Posts • **Route:** `/boost-post-activity`

## The activity table

Each boost appears as a row:

| Column | What it shows |
|---|---|
| Post Author | Who wrote the post you boosted |
| Post Content | The boosted post's text |
| Post URL | Link to the boosted post |
| Activity Status | In progress / Completed |
| Date & Time | When the boost ran |
| Action | **View Boost Activity** — detailed per-boost activity |

## Viewing a boost

Click **View Boost Activity** on a row to see the activity GrowReach performed on that post — comments and interactions added, with timestamps.

<Tip>
  More rows load as you scroll ("Scroll to view more") — no pagination needed.
</Tip>
