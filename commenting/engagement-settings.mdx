# Engagement Settings

Engagement Settings controls **commenting automation** and engagement preferences for your LinkedIn account.

> **Sidebar label:** Engagement Settings • **Route:** `/engagement-settings`

## Commenting switch

The **Commenting** toggle enables or disables commenting on posts entirely. When OFF, GrowReach will not comment on your behalf.

## Commenting Mode

Choose how comments are posted:

| Mode | Behavior |
|---|---|
| **Manual** | The tool suggests comments, but you must approve each one before it's posted |
| **Auto** | Comments are posted automatically without requiring your approval |

1. Go to **Engagement Settings**.
2. Toggle **Commenting** ON (you must enable commenting for modes to apply).
3. Select **Manual** or **Auto**.
4. Click **Save**.

<Tip>
  You also pick your mode first-time during onboarding (Choose Commenting Mode step). This page is where you change it later.
</Tip>
