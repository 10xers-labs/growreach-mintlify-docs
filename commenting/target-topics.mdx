# Target Topics

Target Topics (keywords) tells GrowReach which **topics and keywords** to search for on LinkedIn, so it finds posts — even from creators you haven't targeted yet.

> **Sidebar label:** Target Topics • **Route:** `/keywords`

## How it works

1. GrowReach scans LinkedIn for posts matching your keywords.
2. High-engagement, relevant posts are surfaced for comment generation.
3. This expands your reach into conversations beyond your existing network.

## Adding a topic

1. Go to **Target Topics** in the sidebar.
2. Type **one topic/keyword at a time** into the input (*"Enter Topic/keyword"*).
3. Click **Add** — the topic appears as a chip.
4. Repeat up to **5 topics** (counter `3/5` for example).

## Trending topics

Instead of typing, add a **trending topic** in one click from the list GrowReach shows:

Anthropic • Claude • Open AI • Digital Marketing • Data Analytics • Agentic Workflows • SEO • LLM • AI Solutions • FinTech • SaaS

## Managing your topics

- Click the **✕** on a chip to remove a topic.
- There is no limit on how often you change your topics — save anytime.

## Tips

- Use **specific, niche keywords** — "B2B sales prospecting" beats "sales".
- Combine topic targeting with [Target Linkedin Users](/commenting/target-linkedin-users) for the widest relevant reach.
