# 24/7 Auto Mode

Auto Mode is GrowReach on autopilot: it learns your style and posts thoughtful, contextual comments automatically — in your timezone, while you're working on other things (or sleeping).

## How it works

1. GrowReach finds relevant posts for your targets (accounts + keywords).
2. It generates a comment in your voice.
3. It posts the comment automatically, timed for your timezone.

## Before you switch on Auto Mode

- **Set up your Commenting Voice** — the better your voice configuration, the more on-brand the comments. See [Commenting Voice](/commenting/commenting-voice).
- **Review your Comments Filter** — make sure posts you don't want to comment on (hiring, update, and article posts) are filtered out. See [Comments Filter](/commenting/comments-filter).
- **Start in Manual Mode first** — let GrowReach learn from your corrections for a few days, then switch to auto.

## Staying safe

- GrowReach is designed for human-in-the-loop, safe engagement, with rate and volume safeguards to keep your account healthy.
- You can pause or turn off Auto Mode at any time from the dashboard.
