# Commenting Voice

Your Commenting Voice is how GrowReach makes every comment sound like you. The goal: comments that are indistinguishable from ones you wrote yourself.

## What you configure

### 1. Website of your business

The URL of what you want to indirectly sell. This tells the AI what your product or service is, so it can mention it naturally.

### 2. Target audience

Who you want to attract on LinkedIn (multi-select):

- Consultants and Advisors
- Executives and Business Leaders
- Industry Peers
- Job Seekers and Career Changers
- Press and Media
- Recruiters and HR
- Sales and Marketing Professionals
- Startups
- Technology Enthusiasts

### 3. Content goal

What you want to achieve with your comments (multi-select):

- Brand Awareness
- Build Authority
- Career Opportunities
- Find Potential Partners
- Generate Leads
- Increase Visibility
- Promote Personal Projects
- Share Knowledge

### 4. Content tone

How you want *all* your comments to sound (single-select — tone stays consistent, content differs per post):

- Authoritative
- Conversational
- Educational
- Humorous
- Inspirational
- Personal
- Professional

### 5. Content persona

How you want to be perceived by post authors and viewers (multi-select):

- Community Builder
- Data-Driven Analyst
- Industry Expert
- Innovator
- Mentor/Coach
- Storyteller
- Thought Leader

### 6. Content priority

How to balance authenticity vs performance:

- Authenticity first — metrics matter less
- Authenticity only — true voice above all
- Balance — both equally important
- Metrics first — authenticity matters a bit
- Performance only — reach and engagement

### 7. Emojis

Yes — include emojis in generated comments, or No — no emojis at all.

### 8. Voice brief (auto-generated, editable)

GrowReach analyzes your LinkedIn profile and generates a voice brief pre-filled for you — a paragraph describing your background, expertise, and communication style. You can edit anything to make it more personal. Each voice brief is unique to you.

### 9. Custom instructions (DOs and DON'Ts)

Your own rules for what to highlight and what to avoid. Examples:

**DOs:**
- Use references if possible
- Keep comments between 80 and 150 words
- Invite further engagement with the author or others

**DON'Ts:**
- Don't use curse words
- Don't use em dashes (—), en dashes (–), or hyphens (-)
- Don't make comments longer than 150 words

## How GrowReach learns your style

- **From your profile** — the auto-generated voice brief is based on your LinkedIn profile analysis.
- **From your corrections** — in Manual Mode, every edit you make teaches GrowReach your style.
- **From your existing comments** — if you have existing comments/posts, GrowReach scans them to learn your style.
