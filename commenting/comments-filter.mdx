# Comments Filter

Comments Filter keeps your engagement on-brand by automatically skipping post types that don't fit your professional presence.

## What you can skip

GrowReach lets you toggle three post-type filters (all ON by default):

| Filter | What it skips |
|---|---|
| **Skip Hiring Posts** | Don't comment on job postings or recruitment content |
| **Skip Update Posts** | Avoid personal milestone and status posts |
| **Skip Article Posts** | Don't comment on shared articles or news links |

## How to configure

1. Go to **Comments Filter** in the sidebar (route `/comments-filter`).
2. Toggle each filter **ON** to skip that post type, or **OFF** to allow comments on it.
3. Click **Save**.

## Why it matters

- **Protects your brand** — you never accidentally comment on a hiring post, a personal milestone, or a shared article that doesn't fit your commentary strategy.
- **Saves your credits** — your AI actions go to posts that actually matter for your goals.
- **Keeps you safe** — no engagement on post types that could hurt your professional reputation.

<Tip>
  During onboarding this is where the "skip" behavior is configured. The defaults (all three ON) are the recommended setting — you can change them anytime from the dashboard.
</Tip>
