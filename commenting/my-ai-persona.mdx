# My AI Persona

My AI Persona is the professional profile GrowReach uses to represent you. The better your persona, the more accurate your AI comments — it powers everything from comment generation to the voice brief.

> **Sidebar label:** My AI Persona • **Route:** `/persona-settings`

## Understanding your persona

On the **My AI Persona** page you can edit all the professional attributes GrowReach knows about you:

| Field | Example |
|---|---|
| Your Job Role Or Professional Title | HR/People/Talent |
| Domain/Industry You Work In | Technology, Software and IT Services |
| Business/Product/Company Name | GrowEasy |
| Total Years Of Experience | 3 |
| Seniority Level | Executive |
| Your Domain Expertise/Skills You'd Like To Highlight | Human Resource, Talent Acquisition |
| Website | https://yourwebsite.com |
| A Short Summary Of Your Professional Background (Optional) | *(auto-generated — see below)* |

## The auto-generated background summary

GrowReach generates a summary of your professional background from your LinkedIn profile. It is **crucial for our AI model** — it helps generate comments which correctly represent your experiences, education, and seniority level.

The generated summary is editable — add or change anything you like, then click **Save**.

## Updating your persona

1. Go to **My AI Persona** in the sidebar.
2. Update any field.
3. Click **Save** in the top-right.

<Tip>
  When you update your persona, the AI uses the new professional context for the next comments it generates. Your [Commenting Voice](/commenting/commenting-voice) works together with the persona to shape tone and topics.
</Tip>
