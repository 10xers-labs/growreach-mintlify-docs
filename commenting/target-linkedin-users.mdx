# Target Linkedin Users

Targeting lets you engage with **specific LinkedIn accounts** — prospects, partners, and industry leaders whose posts you want to be visible on.

> **Sidebar label:** Target Linkedin Users • **Route:** `/creator-settings`

## How it works

1. GrowReach monitors the posts of every LinkedIn account you add.
2. When they publish, GrowReach surfaces the post so you can generate a comment (Manual mode) or comments automatically (Auto mode).
3. Your engagement gets you noticed by that account and its followers.

## Adding a LinkedIn user

1. Go to **Target Linkedin Users** in the sidebar.
2. In the search box (*"Search By Name Or Added Target Linkedin Users's Profile URL"*), do one of the following:
   - **Search by name** — type a full name and click **Search**.
   - **Paste a profile URL** — copy the LinkedIn profile link, paste it, and click **Search**.
3. When the profile resolves, it is added to your target list.
4. Repeat up to **20 target LinkedIn users**.

<Tip>
  Not sure how to find a profile URL? Use the **"Check How to find Target Linkedin user's URL"** link right under the search box.
</Tip>

## Managing your list

- Each added user shows as a card with their **avatar, name, and profile URL**.
- Click the **remove (✕)** on a card to stop targeting that user.
- A counter shows your usage: `6/20` for example.

## What to target

Target accounts that matter most:

- **Prospects and dream clients** you want on your radar
- **Industry leaders and influencers** you want to build a relationship with
- **Partners and peers** you want to stay visible to
