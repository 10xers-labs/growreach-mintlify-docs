# Activity

Activity is where all your **topic and target-user comments** land in one place. Review what GrowReach has discovered, approve comments, and keep track of your engagement.

> **Sidebar label:** Activity • **Route:** `/activity`

## What you see

Each activity card shows:

- **Post author** and their headline
- **Post content** (expandable)
- The comment GrowReach generated
- **Status** — Pending (awaiting review) or Published
- **Review** button (in Manual mode) to approve/publish the comment

## Filters

Use the filter bar to find comments fast:

- **Relevance Reason** — why this post matched your targets
- **Date Range** — narrow by time
- **Post Author** — comments from specific authors
- **Status** — Pending / Published

**Clear Filter** resets everything. You can also **search** the activity log.

## Reviewing a comment (Manual mode)

1. Open a Post card with a **Review** button.
2. Read the generated comment.
3. Approve to publish, or edit before publishing.

<Tip>
  In [Manual Mode](/commenting/manual-mode) every comment waits for your approval here. In [Auto Mode](/commenting/auto-mode), comments post automatically — Activity is where you monitor them.
</Tip>
