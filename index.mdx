# Welcome to GrowReach

GrowReach is an AI-powered LinkedIn engagement automation tool that works as your LinkedIn co-pilot — 24/7 while you are busy living life.

It monitors the LinkedIn accounts you specify and searches LinkedIn for keywords you define. It tracks and identifies the most viral and relevant posts for you to comment on, and generates a thoughtful, detailed, human-sounding comment that mentions your product or service and speaks from the relevant experience you've had — showing you as an authority on that topic. Then you can either copy and modify that comment manually, or run GrowReach on auto-mode so it posts thoughtful, contextual comments automatically while you sleep.

The result: you show up consistently in the conversations your ideal customers are already having — building visibility, credibility, and inbound interest without spending hours scrolling LinkedIn every day.

## What GrowReach Is

- **Smart Post Discovery** — AI identifies high-engagement, viral posts in your niche using keyword search and account monitoring
- **AI Comment Generation** — creates contextually relevant, thoughtful comments based on the post content and your business, service, and past experience
- **Manual Mode** — finds the best posts to comment on with a pre-generated suggested comment — you review, modify if needed, and post
- **24/7 Auto Mode** — learns your style and automatically posts thoughtful comments in your timezone while you work on other things (or sleep)
- **Account-based targeting** — comments on specific LinkedIn accounts' posts so you are the first to engage or get visible to them
- **Keyword-based targeting** — AI scans LinkedIn content and finds posts on specific topics and keywords
- **Topic Filtering** — skips posts about religion, politics, personal updates, health updates, and more
- **Learns from your commenting style** — scans your existing comments and posts to learn your style; if you have no existing comments, it learns from your corrections in manual mode

## What GrowReach Is NOT

- Not a post scheduler
- Not a connection spammer (no mass connection requests or cold DMs)
- Not generic automation (every comment is contextually generated, not template-based)
- Not a LinkedIn scraper
- Not a replacement for strategy
