# Best Practices

## Set up your voice before you scale

The single biggest lever on comment quality is your [Commenting Voice](/guides/commenting-voice). Spend 15 minutes configuring your audience, tone, persona, and custom instructions before you turn on Auto Mode. The better the voice, the more on-brand the comments.

## Start in Manual Mode

Run Manual Mode for the first few days. Review every comment, edit the ones that feel off, and let GrowReach learn from your corrections. Then switch to Auto Mode with confidence.

## Target precisely

- Monitor the accounts that matter: prospects, partners, industry leaders.
- Use specific, niche keywords — "B2B sales prospecting" beats "sales".
- Combine account-based and keyword-based targeting for the widest relevant reach.

## Filter aggressively

Keep your [Topic Filters](/features/topic-filtering) on. Skipping religion, politics, personal updates, and health updates protects your brand and saves your credits for posts that matter.

## Comment with value, not promotion

The best comments add to the conversation: a relevant insight, a data point, a question that invites engagement. GrowReach writes comments that mention your product naturally — but the comment should always be valuable to the post's audience first.

## Consistency beats intensity

Showing up consistently in the conversations your ideal customers are already having is what builds visibility, credibility, and inbound interest. A steady stream of thoughtful comments beats occasional bursts.

## Review your analytics

Use the analytics and insights in your dashboard to see which comments and targets drive profile visits and engagement — then double down on what works.
