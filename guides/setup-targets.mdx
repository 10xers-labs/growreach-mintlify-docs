# Setup Targets

Your targets tell GrowReach *whose* posts to find and *what* topics to search. Here's how to set them up.

## Step 1 — Add accounts to monitor (creator targeting)

1. Go to **Targets → Accounts to Monitor**.
2. Click **Add Account**.
3. Search for the LinkedIn profile you want to monitor and select it.
4. Repeat for each account you want to track.

<Tip>
  Your plan includes a set number of target accounts (3 on Starter, 10 on Serious, 30 on Committed). Choose the accounts that matter most: prospects, partners, and industry leaders.
</Tip>

## Step 2 — Add keywords (keyword targeting)

1. Go to **Targets → Keywords**.
2. Enter a keyword or phrase relevant to your niche.
3. Click **Add**.

<Tip>
  Keyword targeting is available on the **Committed** plan (up to 5 keywords). Use specific, niche phrases — "B2B sales prospecting" beats "sales".
</Tip>

## Step 3 — Set your topic filters

1. Go to **Targets → Topic Filters**.
2. Make sure religion, politics, personal updates, and health updates are filtered out.
3. Add any additional topics you want to skip.

## Step 4 — Review your feed

Go to **Posts** and check that the surfaced posts match your targets. Adjust your accounts and keywords until the feed looks right.
