# Installation

GrowReach works through a Chrome extension that connects your browser to your GrowReach dashboard.

## Install the extension

1. Open the [GrowReach Chrome extension](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/growreach-ai-clawd-bot-gr/cifejbakemcfojncacbdakgkbhocglca) in the Chrome Web Store.
2. Click **Add to Chrome**.
3. Confirm with **Add extension** when prompted.

## Log in

1. Click the GrowReach icon in your browser toolbar.
2. Log in with your GrowReach account credentials.
3. The extension connects to your dashboard.

## Verify it's working

- The extension icon should show your account is connected.
- Open your [dashboard](https://dashboard.growreach.app/login) — your LinkedIn account should appear under **Settings → LinkedIn Accounts**.

## Troubleshooting

- **Extension won't install** — make sure you're using a recent version of Chrome or a Chromium-based browser.
- **Not connecting** — log out of the extension and log back in, then refresh the dashboard.
- **Still stuck?** — see [Common Issues](/troubleshooting/common-issues) or [Contact Support](/troubleshooting/contact-support).
