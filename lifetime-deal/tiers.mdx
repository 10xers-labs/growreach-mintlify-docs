# Lifetime Deal Tiers

The lifetime deal has four tiers. Each tier maps to a one-time price and includes a monthly AI credits allowance that renews every month.

|  | Founder | Team | Agency | Agency+ |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Price (one-time)** | \$79 | \$199 | \$399 | \$499 |
| **Connected LinkedIn Accounts** | 1 | 3 | 5 | 10 |
| **Manager Seats** | 1 | 2 | 3 | 5 |
| **Target Prospects** | 10 | 30 | 50 | 200 |
| **Target Topics** | 2 | 9 | 15 | 30 |
| **Total Monthly Credits** | 10,000 | 30,000 | 50,000 | 100,000 |

## Which tier is right for you?

- **Individual (\$79)** — for solo professionals building their personal brand. Using GrowReach to show up consistently rather than expecting full fledged lead pipeline.
- **Team (\$199)** — for a small teams using max 3 connected Linkedin Accounts to comment from and generate leads together under one company account.
- **Agency (\$299)** — built for smaller agencies: 5 connected Linkedin accounts, 3 manager seats, 50,000 AI credits/month, and Team Management features to assign and manage connected Linkedin accounts with managers.
- **Agency+ ($499)** — for serious agencies managing multiple clients or sales teams running 10 connected Linkedin accounts, managed by 5 manager seats, 100,000 AI credits/month.

## Important terms

- **No easy upgrades after purchase** — RocketHub Lifetime deal does not support stacking. Pick the tier your business can grow into. Talk to Rockethub team to change the plan and get a new code
- **Lifetime Deal Activation** — you must use your Rockethub code and activate your license within 60 days of purchase; licenses not activated within 60 days are forfeited. No Exceptions
- **30-day money-back guarantee** — not satisfied? Request a refund via Rockethub within 30 days.
- **Total Al Credits** are allotted each month based on the Tier chosen. These total no of credits are distributed equally among the Connected Linkedin Accounts on that Tier
