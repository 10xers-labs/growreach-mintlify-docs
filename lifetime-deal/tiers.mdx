# Lifetime Deal Tiers

The lifetime deal has four tiers. Each tier maps to a one-time price and includes a monthly credit allowance that renews every month.

| | Individual | Team | Agency | Agency+ |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Price (one-time)** | $99 | $299 | $399 | $499 |
| **LinkedIn Accounts** | 1 | 3 | 5 | 7 |
| **Seats** | 1 | 2 | 3 | 5 |
| **Prospects** | 10 | 30 | 50 | 70 |
| **Topics** | 2 | 9 | 15 | 21 |
| **Monthly Credits** | 15,000 | 30,000 | 45,000 | 75,000 |

## Which tier is right for you?

- **Individual ($99)** — for solo professionals building their personal brand: freelancers, consultants, coaches, founders, and B2B sales reps.
- **Team ($299)** — for small teams running commenting and leads together under one license.
- **Agency ($399)** — built for agencies: 5 accounts, 3 seats, 45k credits/month, and Team Management.
- **Agency+ ($499)** — for agencies managing a lot of clients: 7 accounts, 5 seats, 75k credits/month.

## Add-ons

Need more capacity? Add-ons expand your tier without a new license:

- **Extra credits** — $15 per 1,000 credits
- **Extra accounts** — $30/month
- **Extra seats** — $20/month

## Important terms

- **No upgrades after purchase** — RocketHub does not support changing or upgrading your plan after purchase, and purchase codes can't be combined or stacked. Pick the tier your business can grow into.
- **Activation** — you must activate your license within 60 days of purchase; licenses not activated within 60 days are forfeited.
- **30-day money-back guarantee** — not satisfied? Request a refund within 30 days.
