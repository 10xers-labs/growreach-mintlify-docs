# Lifetime Deal Overview

The GrowReach Lifetime Deal is a one-time purchase that gives you lifetime access to GrowReach — no recurring subscription.

## What you get

- **Lifetime access** to GrowReach's feature modules: Commenting, Leads, Post Boost, and Team Management
- **Monthly credits** that renew every month (10k–100k depending on tier)
- **GrowReach's Custom AI Model** - GrowReach runs its own custom trained AI model so you are not required to use your expensive API keys from Anthropic, OpenAI or others.

## The Feature Modules

1. **Commenting** **Agent** — AI-powered LinkedIn comment Agent thats runs in the cloud
2. **Leads** **Management** — Auto capture and organize prospects from your LinkedIn engagements
3. **Post Boost** — boost your own posts for more reach
   1. **Team Management** — Deploy a dedicated commenting agent and maintain dedicated target prospects, target topics and  and leads for each connected Linkedin account. Manage all accounts with your whole team under 1 admin email

## Tiers

The lifetime deal has four tiers: Individual, Team, Agency, and Agency+. See [Lifetime Deal Tiers](/lifetime-deal/tiers) for the full comparison.

## How it compares to monthly SaaS

GrowReach's monthly SaaS starts at \$19/month and lifetime license from \$79 pays for itself in a few months, and your monthly credits renew every month. See [Lifetime Deal FAQ](/lifetime-deal/faq) for more
