# Lifetime Deal Overview

The GrowReach Lifetime Deal is a one-time purchase that gives you lifetime access to GrowReach — no recurring subscription.

## What you get

- **Lifetime access** to GrowReach's four modules: Commenting, Leads, Post Boost, and Team Management
- **Monthly credits** that renew every month (15k–75k depending on tier)
- **Self-hosted AI** — GrowReach runs its AI on its own hardware, so there are no API keys to manage and no per-use billing surprises
- **Future updates included** — to the extent that new features don't carry a per-use/API cost or substantial hardware/bandwidth cost, they're included in your plan

## The four modules

1. **Commenting** — AI-powered LinkedIn comment generation and posting
2. **Leads** — capture and organize prospects from your LinkedIn engagements
3. **Post Boost** — boost your own posts for more reach
4. **Team Management** — run commenting and leads with your whole team under one license

## Tiers

The lifetime deal has four tiers: Individual, Team, Agency, and Agency+. See [Lifetime Deal Tiers](/lifetime-deal/tiers) for the full comparison.

## How it compares to monthly SaaS

GrowReach's monthly SaaS starts at $19/month. A lifetime license (from $99) pays for itself in a few months, and your monthly credits renew every month. See [Lifetime Deal FAQ](/lifetime-deal/faq) for the value math.
