# Lifetime Deal FAQ

## How do I activate my lifetime deal?

After purchase you'll receive a unique redemption code in your Order Confirmation email, along with redemption instructions. Follow them to redeem your license. You must activate within 60 days of purchase — licenses not activated within 60 days are forfeited.

## What if I'm not 100% satisfied?

We offer a hassle-free 30-day money-back guarantee. Submit a refund request from your My Account dashboard once logged in, or email support with your details, and we'll process the refund as soon as possible. Refund requests after the 30-day period will not be provided.

## Can I upgrade or change my plan tier after purchase?

No. RocketHub does not support changing or upgrading your plan after purchase, and LTD codes cannot be combined or stacked. We recommend purchasing the tier your business can grow into — Individual $99, Team $299, Agency $399, or Agency+ $499. If you need more capacity later, add-ons are available: extra accounts, extra seats, and credit packs.

## Can I stack two purchases for more accounts?

No — per RocketHub terms, purchase codes can't be combined or stacked. Each purchase is its own license. If you need more capacity, add accounts, seats, or credits instead.

## I bought a lower tier — can I upgrade to a higher one later?

No — RocketHub does not support changing or upgrading your plan after purchase, and purchase codes can't be stacked. We recommend purchasing the tier your business can grow into. If you outgrow your tier, add-ons (extra accounts, extra seats, credits) cover the gap.

## I bought a tier but my features/limits don't match what I paid for. What do I do?

If your account doesn't reflect the tier you purchased, contact support and we'll re-permission your account so your accounts, seats, prospects, topics, and credits match your plan. Activation issues are resolved as soon as your license is properly applied.

## Are add-ons included in the deal, or is there a discount for deal buyers?

Add-ons are available at listed prices: extra credits, extra accounts, and extra seats. They don't void your LTD.

## What happens if I hit my plan's limits — are there add-ons, and can I buy another plan?

Your plan's limits (accounts, seats, prospects, topics, credits) are fixed at purchase. Need more? Add-ons expand your capacity without a new license, and add-ons don't void your LTD. Note: RocketHub doesn't support stacking multiple purchase codes.

## Can I keep my LTD while adding workspaces and team members?

Yes — you keep your lifetime deal and add capacity as you grow: extra accounts and extra seats. Team Management includes role-based access so clients can approve and edit while internal staff monitor and audit without touching brand setups.

## How many leads can I capture per month on my tier?

Prospect capacity is 10 (Individual), 30 (Team), 50 (Agency), 70 (Agency+) — that's how many prospects you can hold and work at once, with 15k–75k monthly credits to power the work.

## Is the lifetime deal actually better than the monthly subscription?

Yes. Monthly SaaS is $19 (Starter), $39 (Serious), $79 (Committed) per month — hundreds of dollars a year, forever. A lifetime license (from $99) pays for itself in a few months, and your monthly credits (15k–75k) renew every month. Add-ons are available if you ever need more.

## Why are AI features limited by tier? Isn't that just gatekeeping to push the top tier?

It's not gatekeeping — it's sustainability. GrowReach's AI runs on its own hardware, and that hardware has real capacity. Credits (15k/30k/45k/75k per month by tier) are how we keep the platform fast and stable for every customer, not a way to force upgrades. Every tier includes full AI commenting and lead generation — the tiers differ in volume, not access. If you need more volume, add-on credits are available.

## Are the tier limits enough for my use case?

GrowReach's tiers are sized for real usage: accounts 1/3/5/7, seats 1/2/3/5, prospects 10/30/50/70, topics 2/9/15/21, and monthly credits 15k/30k/45k/75k. If you outgrow a tier, add-ons expand it without a new license.

## Which tier is right for an agency?

Agency ($399) and Agency+ ($499) tiers are built for agencies: 5–7 accounts, 3–5 seats, 45k–75k credits/month, and Team Management. Agency+ if you manage a lot of clients.

## What kind of support is included with the lifetime deal?

Lifetime deal buyers get direct support from the GrowReach team, plus a knowledge base and tutorials.

## Will I get access to future updates?

Yes. To the extent that new features do not have a per-use cost, API usage, or substantial hardware/bandwidth cost to us, they will be included in your plan. Our lifetime deal plans map to our standard plans on the website. We may update pricing, limits, and/or names of our public plans in the future, but we will always honor the plans and limits shown here for lifetime deal license holders.
