# Lifetime Deal Credits

A Credit is the unit or currency you spend to use the features on GrowReach , be it your Agent commenting on your behalf, you boosting and getting community engagement on your post o r generating context rich connection request messages for the leads you got. All actions that help you succeed on Linkedin use credits. Every plan includes a monthly credit allowance that renews each month.

## Why credits?

At the core GrowReach is an AI product with everything it does to get you warm leads on Linkedin. We've custom trained our own open-weights model that specialises in posting comments that get noticed and engaged with. 

Credits are the currency for the entire AI operation and an easy way to account for the AI inference generations that it costs us to do these operations. Each AI generation costs inference, hardware usage and compute to generate great quality comments that get you the end outcome - Warm Leads for your business.

## What consumes credits

Every AI action — generating a comment, analyzing a prospect, boosting a post — consumes credits. Manual actions don't consume credits.

| Credit Usage | Credits Used | Estimated Range |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Contextual comments posted on target Linkedin Posts | Depends on post content and comment length | 50-100 |
| Post Boost ( community likes & comments to boost your post ) | Depends on the post length and engagement done | 200-300 |
| Lead Connection Request Note | Depends on the context of last warm interaction | 50-100 |
| Reply To Prospect's Comment | Depends on post content and reply comment length | 50-100 |

> Note: Credit usage depends on the length of the content and the model being used to generate a hyper personalised and humane output. From time to time we upgrade the models as new open weight models come out and as result of our eval testing on the quality of output. <br />Our primary goal is that the comments sound so humane and drive leads for our customers. So these estimated credit usage range can change as we use bigger models to generate better output

## Do credits roll over?

No — monthly credits renew each month and do not roll over, you get fresh credits each month for lifetime based on the Tier you purchased.

## What happens when I hit my limit?

Your monthly credits reset each month. If you need more headroom in a given month, top up with add-on credit packs.
