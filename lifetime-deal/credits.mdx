# Lifetime Deal Credits

Credits are the monthly capacity your GrowReach agent uses to engage on LinkedIn. Every plan includes a monthly credit allowance that renews each month.

## Monthly allowances by tier

| Tier | Monthly Credits |
|---|---|
| Individual | 15,000 |
| Team | 30,000 |
| Agency | 45,000 |
| Agency+ | 75,000 |

## What consumes credits

Every AI action — generating a comment, analyzing a prospect, boosting a post — consumes credits. Manual actions don't consume credits.

## Do credits roll over?

No — monthly credits renew each month and do not roll over, so plan your commenting and lead volume accordingly.

## What happens when I hit my limit?

Your monthly credits reset each month. If you need more headroom in a given month, top up with add-on credit packs.

## Add-on credit packs

- **$15 per 1,000 credits** — purchased as needed from your billing page.

## Why credits?

GrowReach's AI runs on its own hardware, and that hardware has real capacity. Credits are how we keep the platform fast and stable for every customer — not a way to force upgrades. Every tier includes full AI commenting and lead generation; the tiers differ in volume, not access.
