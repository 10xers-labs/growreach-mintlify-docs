# Topic Filtering

Topic Filtering keeps your engagement on-brand by automatically skipping posts that don't fit your professional presence.

## What it filters

GrowReach skips posts about topics that are risky or irrelevant for professional engagement, including:

- Religion
- Politics
- Personal updates
- Health updates

## Why it matters

- **Protects your brand** — you never accidentally comment on a controversial or off-topic post
- **Saves your credits** — your AI actions go to posts that actually matter for your goals
- **Keeps you safe** — no engagement on sensitive topics that could hurt your reputation

## Availability

Topic Filtering is included on the **Serious** and **Committed** plans.
