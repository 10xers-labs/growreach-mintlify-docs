# Smart Post Discovery

Finding the right posts to comment on is the hardest part of LinkedIn engagement. GrowReach's Smart Post Discovery does it for you.

## How it works

GrowReach continuously scans LinkedIn for two kinds of content:

1. **Posts from accounts you monitor** — the specific LinkedIn profiles you want to engage with (creator targeting). GrowReach watches their posts so you can be among the first to comment.
2. **Posts matching your keywords** — LinkedIn content on the topics and keywords you define (keyword targeting). This surfaces posts from creators you haven't targeted yet, expanding your reach into new conversations.

## What makes a post "relevant"

GrowReach ranks candidate posts by engagement and relevance to your niche, so you see the highest-opportunity posts first — not a firehose of everything on LinkedIn.

## Getting the most out of it

- Add the accounts that matter most to you (prospects, partners, industry leaders) to your monitor list.
- Use specific, niche keywords rather than broad ones — "B2B sales prospecting" beats "sales".
- Combine both targeting types: monitor key accounts *and* keyword-search the wider conversation.
