# Manual Mode

Manual Mode puts you in control: GrowReach finds the best posts and pre-generates a suggested comment, but nothing goes out until you say so.

## How it works

1. GrowReach surfaces the highest-engagement, most relevant posts for your targets.
2. For each post, it shows a suggested comment written in your voice.
3. You review the comment, modify it if you want, and post it yourself — or copy it to post later.

## Why use Manual Mode

- **Full control** — nothing is posted without your approval
- **Learning** — GrowReach learns from your corrections in manual mode, so the more you use it, the better its suggestions match your style
- **Confidence** — ideal when you're starting out or when a post is sensitive

## Tips

- Don't just accept suggestions — edit them. Every correction teaches GrowReach your style.
- Use manual mode for high-value targets (key prospects, partners) and auto mode for the rest.
