# Contact Support

We're here to help. Support options depend on your plan.

## Support by plan

| Plan | Support |
|---|---|
| Starter | Self-serve help center |
| Serious | Priority Email |
| Committed | Priority Chat |

## Self-serve help center

Browse this documentation — the [Quickstart](/quickstart), [Guides](/guides/setup-targets), and [Common Issues](/troubleshooting/common-issues) cover most questions.

## Email support

For priority email support, email us from the account associated with your GrowReach subscription. Include:

- Your account email
- Your plan
- A description of the issue
- Any error messages or screenshots

## Chat support

Committed plan customers get priority chat support from the dashboard.

## What to include when contacting us

To help us resolve your issue faster:

1. Your account email
2. Your plan (Starter, Serious, or Committed — or your lifetime deal tier)
3. What you were doing when the issue occurred
4. Any error messages or screenshots
5. Your browser and operating system (for extension issues)
