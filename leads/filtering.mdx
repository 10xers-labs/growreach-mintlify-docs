# Filter & Search Leads

The Leads page gives you multiple ways to find the right leads fast.

> **Route:** `/leads` — use the controls at the top of the page

## Date range

Select **All** or **Last 30 Days** to narrow the list by time.

## Sorting

Choose how leads are ordered:

- **Hottest** — highest heat score first
- **Newest** — most recent engagement first
- **Most engaged** — most interactions with your comments
- **Best ICP fit** — best match to your ideal customer profile (ICP)

## Search

Use the search box (*"Search by name, headline or topic..."*) to find a specific person or topic.

## Stat tile filters

Click any stat tile to jump to that subset — e.g. **Hot Leads** filters the list to heat-score-75+ leads instantly.
