# Act on Your Leads

Taking action on a warm lead moves them from a name in the list to a real relationship.

> **Route:** `/leads`

## The pipeline (Kanban view)

Leads move through stages as you act on them:

- **All** — every lead
- **New** — leads you haven't acted on yet
- **Reached out** — you've sent a connection request or message
- **Connected** — you're connected on LinkedIn
- **Won** — the lead became a customer or won deal
- **Skipped** — you passed on this lead

Change a lead's stage from its card — click the pipeline segment at the top to filter by stage.

<Tip>
  The pipeline reflects how warm the relationship is. Your goal is to move leads from **New** → **Reached out** → **Connected** → **Won**.
</Tip>

## Lead card actions

- **View more** — expand the lead's details (their post context, matched topics, company).
- **Reach out** — send a personalized connection request based on the commenting interaction and post topic.
