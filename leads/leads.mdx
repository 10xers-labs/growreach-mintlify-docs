# Leads

Leads are **people who engaged with your LinkedIn comments** — profiles that visited you, reacted, or replied. Leads are ranked by how warm they are right now, so you focus on the hottest opportunities first.

> **Sidebar label:** Leads • **Route:** `/leads`

## The stat tiles

At the top of the Leads page:

| Tile | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Total Leads | Everyone who engaged with you |
| People Engaged | Profiles who interacted with your comments |
| Hot Leads | High-intent leads — **heat score 75+**, tagged **HOT** |
| Questions | People who asked you something |
| This Week | New leads in the last 7 days |

## Reading a lead card

Each lead card shows:

- **Name** and **headline**
- **Date** of engagement
- **Pipeline stage** — New / Reached out / Connected / Won / Skipped
- **Heat score** (with a **HOT** badge when 75+)
- **Industry** and **matched topics** — e.g. *"On Supratim Sarkar's post about …"*
- **Actions** — **View more** and **Reach out**
