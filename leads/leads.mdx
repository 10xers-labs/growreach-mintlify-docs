# Leads

Leads are **people who engaged with your LinkedIn comments** — profiles that visited you, reacted, or replied. Leads are ranked by how warm they are right now, so you focus on the hottest opportunities first.

> **Sidebar label:** Leads • **Route:** `/leads`

## Understanding the Lead Score

Every lead gets a **Lead Score (0–100)** that measures how warm they are — how likely they are worth your time right now. A higher score means a hotter lead.

The score is built from the **quality of the interaction** that caught your lead, not just its existence. The factors that matter:

- **What they did** — a thoughtful reply or a question signals more interest than a simple like or reaction.
- **How often they engage** — a prospect who interacts with your comments repeatedly is warmer than a one-off visitor.
- **How recently they engaged** — recent activity counts; interest cools with time.
- **How well they match your ICP** — prospects who fit the [target audience you defined](/leads/icp) rank as better leads.

A lead matching your ICP well *and* engaging strongly is your ideal win — that's the combination that scores as a **Hot Lead**.

## Hot Leads (score 75+)

Leads with a **Lead Score of 75 or above** are marked **HOT** on their card — these are your strongest opportunities and should be your priority for [reaching out](/leads/actions).

## Reading a lead card

Each lead card shows:

- **Name** and **headline**
- **Date** of engagement and **interaction frequency**
- **Pipeline stage** — New / Reached out / Connected / Won / Skipped
- **Lead Score** (with a **HOT** badge when 75+)
- **ICP tags** — e.g. *"Matches your ICP"* alongside the interaction tags (like *"Replied"*)
- **Industry** and **matched topics** — e.g. *"On Supratim Sarkar's post about …"*
- **Actions** — **View more** and **Reach out**

The **Lead Score is the most prominent element** on the card — it's the fastest signal of which lead deserves your attention next.
