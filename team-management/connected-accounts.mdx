# Connected Accounts

Connected Accounts is where your LinkedIn connection with GrowReach lives.

> **Sidebar label:** Connected Accounts (individual) • **Route:** `/connected-account`

<Warning>
  **You can only connect one LinkedIn account.** To add a different account, delete the current one first.
</Warning>

## What you see

A table with your connected account details:

| Column | What it shows |
|---|---|
| Account | The connected LinkedIn account |
| Account ID | Your account's unique ID |
| Status | Connected |
| Connected On | Date the connection was made |
