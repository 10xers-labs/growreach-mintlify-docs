Manage your subscription, billing details, and payment methods — and redeem your Lifetime Deal code.

</Tip>

## Redeeming an LTD code

<Steps>
  1. Click **Redeem LTD Code**.
  2. In the modal, enter your **Redemption Code**.
  3. Click **Redeem Code** (or **Cancel**).
</Steps>
